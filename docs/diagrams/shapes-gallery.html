The five shapes, found in three years of gold

Pin bar, doji, belt-hold, engulfing and tweezers — the five this project decided a trader actually reads. Here is the clearest real example of each, with the candles either side, and how often each turned up.

Real candles. 4,165 XAU/USD 4-hour candles from Twelve Data, 23 October 2023 to 14 August 2026 · weekends and holidays removed · every shape found with the thresholds already written in config/ta.toml · ATR worked out as it goes, so each candle is judged against how big a normal candle was at the time.

A shape every twelve candles is not a signal. It is a description. What makes one worth a message is the level it printed at, and that is not this crate's question.

One candle

Measured from its own proportions alone. Nothing to the left of it matters.

Two candles

These need the one before. That is the whole difference — and it is why the code splits candle/ from pattern/. A shape you can read from one candle and a shape that needs two are different questions.

Three things these pictures settle

  • Hammer and inverted hammer are not separate shapes. A hammer is a bullish pin bar. The textbook separates them by what came before — a hammer follows a downtrend — and that is context, not shape. Context belongs to nsc-strategy.
  • One candle can be several things. The 7 June 2024 candle below is both a bearish engulfing and a tweezer top. Both are true. Deciding which matters needs the level, and that is not this crate's job.
  • Two yardsticks, not one. Shape is measured as shares of the candle's own height, so a fifth is a fifth on gold and on the euro. Size is measured in ATR. Only the belt-hold and the tweezer tolerance need ATR, and both are about size rather than shape.

The thresholds are textbook defaults, taken on 12 August 2026 so the detectors could be built. Nobody has chosen them yet. What replaces them is a pair of charts — one setup taken and one passed that looked the same.
