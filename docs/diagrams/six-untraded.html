The six he does not trade

Every pattern nsc-ta can name but rung 3 ignores — two real examples of each, found by the actual detector on saved candles. The shaded candles are the pattern, the faded ones are context, and the dashed line is the price the pattern is about.

REAL CANDLES. Interactive Brokers, five pairs, 1-hour and 4-hour. Each example was found by pattern::ending_at with config/patterns.toml as it stands today — so what is drawn is what the code calls it, not a textbook picture. The two examples of each come from different pairs, so a pair is never two views of one afternoon.

bullish harami

reversal, weak

A big down candle, then a small one that hides inside its body. The selling did not reverse — it ran out.

bearish harami

reversal, weak

A big up candle, then a small one inside its body. The buying ran out. The same shape, other way up.

tweezer bottom

reversal at a level

Two candles with almost exactly the same low. Price was bought twice at one price.

tweezer top

reversal at a level

Two candles reaching almost exactly the same high. Refused twice at one price.

piercing line

reversal

A down candle, then an up candle that closes back more than halfway into it. More than a bounce, less than an engulfing.

dark cloud cover

reversal

An up candle, then a down candle closing more than halfway back into it. The mirror of the piercing line.

morning star

reversal, 3 candles

A long fall, then a candle where nothing happened, then a long rise. The pause is the point — that is where the move gave up.

evening star

reversal, 3 candles

A long rise, a stall, then a long fall.

three white soldiers

continuation

Three strong up candles in a row, each closing higher. Not a reversal — a statement that one side is winning.

three black crows

continuation

Three strong down candles in a row, each closing lower.

The abandoned baby is not here. It is the rarest of the star family — a gap on both sides of the middle candle — and spot forex does not gap, so the sweep found none at all. The code can name it and probably never will.

WHY NONE OF THESE FIRE TODAY. Rung 3 accepts two shapes: your push-then-pin and the engulfing. These six are named because they are on every candlestick page, not because they are on your chart. Nothing here has been measured — there is no evidence any of them is worth adding, and none that they are not.
