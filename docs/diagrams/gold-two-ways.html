Why they differ so much

You said a run has to reach about half of the previous run. I built it as half the biggest of the last five, to stop a ratchet where each leg is 60% of the last and the chain shrinks to nothing.

On real gold that backfires. One enormous run poisons the memory, and because rejected runs never enter the memory, the giant never ages out. In 83 days of 4-hour gold it found two swings — which is why there are no levels at all: a level needs two touches at the same price, and two swings 358 points apart give you nothing.

Measuring against the middle keeps the ratchet protection — a shrinking chain drags the middle down slowly, not instantly — while one freak move no longer silences the next three months.
