How hard price left

A level that launched a big move is more convincing than one price wandered away from. Measured as: how far did price travel after the touch, in normal candles.

What I deliberately left out

Anything that needs a guess about what happens next. "This level looks like it will break" is not a measurement — it is a prediction, and it belongs in the strategy, not in the chart reading.

The split matters because chart reading has to give the same answer every time, for the backtester and the live bot alike. Predictions can be wrong; measurements cannot.
