The two cannot both be true

Same pen everywhere. You draw with one line width, so the band looks the same on every chart. That is what I measured: 80 pips on USDCAD weekly, and on gold 90 points. Both about 2.3% of the screen.

Same share of a normal candle. Gold's weekly band works out at 0.35 of a normal weekly candle. Apply that to USDCAD and you get 49 pips, not 80.

What the file holds now

One price per level, not a top and a bottom. The band is worked out from a normal candle when the level is loaded.

Two reasons. The thickness is the same on every chart you draw, so it is a setting rather than something to write out a hundred times. And reading one centre line off a screenshot is accurate to about 3 points; reading two edges was accurate to about 15.

The question

Look at the weekly bands on the second chart — 1.3815 to 1.3865 around your 1.3840 line. Is that the band you would have drawn, or is it too tight?
