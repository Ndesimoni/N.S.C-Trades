Two things the rule has to get right

Both of these are tested. Neither is obvious.

A hidden level cannot hide another

edge case

The daily overlaps the weekly, so it loses its line. The 4-hour overlaps the daily but sits clear of the weekly. It keeps its line — otherwise that price would have no line at all, hidden behind something that is itself hidden.

What counts as the same price

one setting

bands_overlap is your answer: they touch at all, the smaller one goes. A weekly band is thick, so it will swallow a fair few dailies. centre_inside is the looser reading, in ta.toml if it turns out to hide too much.

Nothing is deleted

A hidden level keeps its band, its touches, its dates and its own timeframe tag. It is marked, not dropped.

That matters more than the drawing does. Two timeframes turning at one price is confluence — the reason the price is worth trading at all. Delete the daily level and the strategy loses the reason the weekly one is good.

Why the chart stays light in dark mode

Black means weekly. That is a specification, not a design choice — it is how you colour your own charts. Black only reads as black on a light ground, so the chart panels keep one in both themes, the way a chart does.

This is an argument, not evidence

The prices above were chosen to make the rule clear. They show what the code does. They say nothing about whether these are the levels worth trading — that needs real candles, and after that, judged signals.
