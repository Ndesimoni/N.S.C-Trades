Diagram · two-candle pattern · proposed

Push then pin

Every combination of the setup, drawn as pairs. A momentum candle that shows one side winning, then a pin bar whose tail points against the push and runs at least twice its own body.

The tail opposes the push. Push up needs a tail down; push down needs a tail up. The tail is a pullback that failed — which is what makes this a continuation pattern rather than a reversal one.
Made-up candles throughout, drawn from the thresholds in config/candles.toml and named by a mirror of the running order in naming.rs. The gold pair near the bottom is real and marked so. A diagram is an argument, not evidence — none of this says the pattern makes money.
The two halves vary independently: any of the four momentum candles works with any of the six pin bars. So each is shown in full with the other held steady, rather than printing all twenty-four crossings twice over.

Yours, and one number stands in the way

marubozu  +  plain

tail = 2.00x body

No top at all, and a tail exactly twice the body — the biggest body your rule allows. By your rule it is in. The code calls it plain and will not name it a rejection at all.

One setting does it. The body works out at 0.3333 of the candle and body.small is set at 0.33. Nothing else blocks it — the tail passes, the nose is zero. Move that number to 0.3334 and this candle is named the same as every other pin bar above.

Worth knowing before it moves: body.small is not private to this pattern. It decides what counts as a small body for every candle on every pair, so widening it renames candles far outside this setup. A setting that belongs to the pattern alone would live in config/patterns.toml instead.

How big the body can get

With no top, the body and the tail are the whole candle. If the tail must be twice the body, then body + 2·body fills it — so the body can never be more than 1/3. That is arithmetic, not a setting you can loosen.

Ask for a bigger body and you are asking for a shorter tail:

Turned round, the same sum gives the ceiling for any multiple you pick: a 3x tail caps the body at 0.25, a 4x tail at 0.20.

Where this stands

  • The pieces exist; the pair does not. nsc-ta names both halves and config/candles.toml already carries the 2x rule as rejection.tail_to_body. Nothing joins them.
  • The finder does not look for this. It finds twelve — engulfing and harami both ways, tweezer top and bottom, piercing line, dark cloud cover, morning and evening star, three white soldiers, three black crows. Not this one.
  • Direction is the whole rule. Tail against the push is the pattern; tail with the push is a different animal. A detector that ignored which way the tail pointed would fire on twice as much and mean half as much.
  • One shape, two names. The code splits the pin bar by body size — under 0.05 it is a dragonfly or gravestone doji, above that a long lower or upper wick. A detector has to accept all four or it will miss the cleanest ones.
  • One number to settle. body.small at 0.33 turns away the largest body the rule allows. 0.3334 admits it — but that setting is shared by every candle everywhere, so the pattern may want its own instead.
  • No level, no meaning. A pin bar in open space is noise. Gold's black zone is still not in config/pairs/XAUUSD.toml.
