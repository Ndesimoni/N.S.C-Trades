How Close Counts

The alert fires when price gets near one of his bands. How near? The question looked like a choice between a fixed number of pips and a share of the band. Measured on his own three pairs, it turned out to be neither — because the band is already the early warning.

Real data · XAU/USD, GBP/USD, EUR/USD · Twelve Data, 15 Aug 2026

Start from the line he actually drewthe finding

A level is a band, and his gold weekly band is 77 points thick. So price touching its outer edge is already a long way from the line in the middle. Measured in hours of normal movement rather than points, that gap is the whole argument.

So both framings of the question were wrong

A share of the band

Travels between instruments, which is why it is right for the band's own thickness. But used again for the approach it double-counts — the band was already sized by how much the pair moves.

Result: the thicker the zone, the earlier the alert. Backwards. A thick zone is already plenty of notice.

A fixed 8 pips

A pip on gold is ten cents, and gold moves $13 in a normal hour — so eight pips is six minutes. On the euro the same eight pips is nearly two hours. Twenty-six times apart.

Exactly what CLAUDE.md already warns about: a pip setting works on the pair you tested and quietly stops working on every other one.

The catch is that the second objection stops mattering when the number is small enough. Six minutes of warning is useless as warning — but perfectly good as rounding, which is all that was needed once the band turned out to be doing the warning.

Settled: one pip, and it comes from digitssettled

Price at 4132.60 against a top of 4132.57 has touched the level. Staying quiet over three cents would be silly, and that is the entire job.

Nothing new to configure

A pip is ten ticks, so it falls straight out of digits — which every pair file already has, and which the bot works out for itself when he sends a new pair from his phone.

One setting, approach_pips = 1.0, meaning the same thing on gold and on the euro.

Arriving and leaving are different sums

Easy to trigger, hard to reset. A touch fires. But the band stays quiet until price is a tenth of its thickness clear — 7.7 points on gold.

One pip each way would turn a single visit into an afternoon of alerts: a pip out, a pip back, over and over.

The measurement was wrong the first timeworth keeping

The first run said gold moves 0.73 in a normal hour, which made a fixed 8 pips look like a full hour of warning and nearly settled the question the other way.

It was measured on a Saturday. Most of the last forty hourly candles were weekend — near-zero range, because the market is shut — and averaging them in said the market was calm when it was closed.

Gold actually moves 13.33 an hour. That is the weekend-candle problem already written down in PROGRESS.md — still not handled in code, and it has now cost one wrong answer inside ten minutes.

This is an argument, not a result. It settles what "close enough" means. Whether the alerts are worth acting on needs a month of them and a record of what happened next.

In code: approach_pips = 1.0 in config/levels.toml, the pip from each pair's digits, and the clearance a tenth of the band. Thirty-three tests in nsc-core/src/levels/tests/.
