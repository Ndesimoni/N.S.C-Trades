The one that got absorbed, close up

January to August 2026. The weekly band runs 4330–4455; the daily sits at 4368–4416, entirely inside it. Both were touched three times.

Why it goes

The daily band is completely inside the weekly one. Not near it — inside it. Drawing both would put a thin blue line in the middle of a thick black one.

You said it yourself: look at the black line and you already know that price matters. A second line there says nothing new.

Note this case would be absorbed under any setting. The absorb_gap_bands = 0.5 clearance only starts mattering for levels that sit near a bigger band without touching it — and there are none of those in this file.

Something to look at: eight weekly, two daily

That is the wrong way round for a daily chart, and it is max_age_bars = 500 doing it.

Five hundred candles means about two years on the daily but nearly ten years on the weekly — longer than this file. So the weekly search reads all six years while the daily only reads the last two, and the daily comes back nearly empty.

Counting in candles was meant to stop weekends shortening the lookback. It does that, but it also makes "how far left do I look" mean something different on every timeframe. Worth deciding whether you want the same number of candles, or the same stretch of time.

No 4-hour levels here

This file is daily candles, and you cannot cut a daily candle into 4-hour ones. Export XAUUSD at 30 minutes and the same command draws all three colours.

What this does and does not show

It shows the rule running on real gold: which levels the code finds, and which lose their line. That part is now checkable by eye.

It says nothing about whether these are the levels worth trading. That needs your judgement on them, and after that, judged signals. The question for you is simpler: are these the lines you would have drawn?
