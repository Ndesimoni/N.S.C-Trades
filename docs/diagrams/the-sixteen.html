What this does not say

  • A pattern is a description, not a signal. Every one of these says two or three candles did a thing together. None of them says the thing means anything — that depends on the level it printed at, and none was involved here.
  • No trend. Swings are not wired in, so nothing here can tell a continuation from a reversal.
  • One pattern per candle. Real runs sit in more than one family at once. The longest and strictest wins, so these counts are what was REPORTED, not everything that was true.
  • Only his own has been measured. nsc-bull and nsc-bear were followed for ten candles and did not continue. The other fourteen have never been checked at all.
  • Nothing here was tuned to make a pattern appear. The counts are what the rules produce, not a target they were fitted to. A threshold loosened until a shape turns up is a rule fitted to a wish, and it is the fastest way to a backtest that looks good and trades badly.
