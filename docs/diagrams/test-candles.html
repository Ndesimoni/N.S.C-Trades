The three candles the tests are built on

Every test in candle/tests.rs uses a candle that actually printed on gold. Here they are, with the hours either side — because a candle on its own tells you the shape, and the hours around it tell you what it meant.

Real candles. XAU/USD from Twelve Data. The one under test is drawn solid with its stamp beneath; its neighbours are faded. Nothing here is invented — these are the exact open, high, low and close the tests assert on.

Test · a_real_doji_is_almost_all_wick

The doji — 15 May 2026, 16:00

It travelled $23.16 and finished 31 cents from where it started. Up nine dollars, down fourteen, back to the middle. Nobody won the hour.

body0.0136
upper0.5619
lower0.4245
reach1.16 ATR

Look at what came after. The indecision was real — price went nowhere for hours. That is what the shape was saying, and it is also why a doji on its own is not a signal. It says nobody won, not buy.

Test · a_marubozu_is_all_body_and_no_wick

The marubozu — 21 March 2026, 00:00

Opened at 4679.65, which was its high. Closed at 4615.05, which was its low. Sixty-four dollars straight down and not one tick of wick at either end.

body1.0000
upper0.0000
lower0.0000
reach3.23 ATR

This is the opposite end of the same measurement. The body is the whole candle, so both wicks are nothing — the only candle in 5,000 that measures exactly 1.0000, which is why it is the one the test uses. And reach at 3.23 says it was more than three normal candles wide, so nobody has to wonder whether it mattered.

Test · a_flat_weekend_candle_has_no_shape

The candle that is not one — 19 April 2025, 07:00

Easter Saturday. Open, high, low and close are all 3326.27. The feed sends it anyway, and it looks like a single flat line.

body—
upper—
lower—
reach—

There is nothing to divide by. Its range is zero, so every share would be zero over zero. Shape::of answers None rather than a number that looks fine and means nothing.

Not a rare accident. Seven candles in 5,000 are exactly this, and 1,412 hourly candles have a range under 0.02% of price. They are the reason the very first Choppiness run reported price travelling 5,119% of its own box.

Why real candles and not made-up ones

Invented numbers only prove the arithmetic. A real candle also proves the arithmetic survives what the feed actually sends — five decimal places on an instrument quoted to two, a candle with no range at all, and a body that measures exactly one.

All three are in crates/nsc-ta/src/candle/tests.rs, with their stamps in the comments so anyone can pull them up and check.
