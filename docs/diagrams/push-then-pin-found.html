How the rule got here

Worth keeping, because the numbers moved a long way and the reason matters.

rulefoundwhat changed
shape only — candle 1 mostly body146 no size test at all
+ candle 1 at least a normal candle79 dropped 67 where the push had no force in it
+ big bodies allowed up to the 1/3 limit82 added back 3 the body cap was turning away
The size floor is what made the grade unnecessary. An earlier draft split these into strong and weak. But 45% of the 146 had a "momentum" candle smaller than a normal one, and calling those strong would have been a name promising something nothing tested. Excluding them outright says the same thing and cannot be misread — the smallest candle 1 in these 82 is 1.0x, the median 1.5x.

Before you believe any of that

The outcome numbers above are the weakest thing on this page. Six reasons, and the first two are the project's own rules.

  • Eighty is not enough. The backtest rule kept in this project says under about a hundred trades it is "a story with a decimal point". This is eighty, and every breakdown row is a fraction of that — fifteen here, seven there. Read the direction, never the decimals.
  • No spread, no slippage. Nothing was taken off for the cost of getting in and out. Real trading only makes these numbers worse, never better.
  • The +1 / -1 test is mine, not yours. You have never said where the stop or the target goes. I picked one normal candle either side because it is symmetric and cannot flatter the result. A different stop and target could say something quite different — that is a real question, not a technicality.
  • No level was involved. This is the big one. All eighty are shapes in open space, and the whole idea of this project is that a pin bar means something at a level you drew. You have never claimed the shape works on its own. What this measures is not the way you trade.
  • The pair you circled is not in these numbers. Gold's daily on 20 August closed yesterday, so there are not ten days after it yet. It and one 4-hour are the two that had to be dropped. Whatever it does next, it did not vote here.
  • The windows are lopsided. The 30-minute reaches back eight days and the 1-hour sixteen. Half the sample is a fortnight of one market. A quiet fortnight that kept reversing would tilt all of this on its own, and nothing here can rule that out.
What it is fair to take from this. The shape alone, with no level under it, did not predict continuation on this data — and it leaned the other way consistently enough to be worth a look. That is not the same as "the pattern does not work". It is the same thing this project has said from the start: a shape is a description, and what makes it worth anything is the level it printed at. The obvious next test is these eighty against your levels, once gold's black zone is in config/pairs.
